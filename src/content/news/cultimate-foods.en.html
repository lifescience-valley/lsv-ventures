BERLIN, 22 April 2024 – Cultimate Foods, a biotech start-up based at the Berlin Biocube and in Hannover (Institute of Technical Chemistry, Leibniz University Hannover), has successfully closed its seed round to scale up its production processes and expand its commercial collaborations and operations.

The EUR 2.3 million financing round was led by High-Tech Gründerfonds (HTGF), one of Europe’s leading seed investors. Alongside HTGF, the Life Science Valley Growth Fund, b.value AG and Kale United took part. Big Idea Ventures, the US-based pre-seed investor of the start-up, also participated in this round.

Cultimate Foods has developed an alternative fat that gives plant-based meat substitutes an authentic taste. Although there are many plant-based alternatives on supermarket shelves, taste is still the biggest argument that keeps consumers from choosing these sustainable products. As a B2B partner, Cultimate Foods supplies cell-cultivated premium fats to the alternative meat industry to solve this problem. The company has already carried out several commercial pilot projects with leading companies from the food industry.

Cultimate Foods took its first steps in Göttingen at the Life Science Factory, where it was able to kick-start its research in the superbly equipped laboratories straight after the pre-seed round.

“Science is the key to solving many of the food industry’s problems. With innovative technologies, we can deliver the meat taste that consumers want while curbing unethical factory farming and countering climate change by reducing CO2 emissions. Follow your passion and make the world better,” says Eugenia Sagué, Co-founder and Managing Director of Cultimate Foods.

“In a short time, Cultimate Foods has reached important milestones, built strong relationships with the foodtech industry and established collaborations with universities. HTGF is pleased to open up a new investment field and to support Cultimate Foods in revolutionising the alternative meat industry with its cell-cultivated fat ingredient. We have the chance to make a real difference in the food industry,” says Tilmann Petersen, Investment Manager at HTGF.

Caroline Mak, Senior Director at Big Idea Ventures, comments: “As one of the most active investors in agri- and food technology, we regard innovation in fats as crucial to improving the taste, health profile, sustainability and scalability of sustainable foods. As early pre-seed investors in Cultimate who have witnessed the team’s development, we are delighted to continue supporting Cultimate in this successful seed round as it brings its innovative fat ingredient to market.”

“Closing the seed round in today’s fundraising environment confirms the success of our technological approach and the efforts of our team. With a consortium of experienced biotech and foodtech funds on board, we look forward to benefiting from both areas of expertise as we scale up to the next TRL and open up our first markets,” says George Zheleznyi, Co-founder and Managing Director of Cultimate Foods.

About Cultimate Foods

Cultimate Foods is a cellular agriculture company that produces alternative fat for plant-based meat products. Unlike other fat alternatives, the company creates a unique lipid composition of the fat that delivers an authentic pork and beef taste. The company has so far raised more than EUR 3 million and is backed by the strong life science investors HTGF, b.value AG and the Life Science Valley Growth Fund, as well as the largest foodtech funds Big Idea Ventures, ProVeg International and Kale United. The seed investor High-Tech Gründerfonds (HTGF) finances technology start-ups with growth potential and has supported more than 700 start-ups since 2005.
