Göttingen, 9 August 2024 – Digity GmbH, a spin-off of the University Medical Center Göttingen (UMG), has announced the closing of its seed financing round of EUR 1.4 million. The investment is backed by renowned venture capital investors from Lower Saxony and Thuringia as well as experienced business angels from across Germany: NBank Capital Beteiligungsgesellschaft, bm-t beteiligungsmanagement thüringen gmbh (bm|t), Life Science Valley Ventures Management GmbH from Göttingen, Mittelständische Beteiligungsgesellschaft Niedersachsen (MBG) mbH from Hannover, business angel Christoph Höynck from Münster, and 4SeedImpact Business Angel GmbH from Chemnitz. The funds will be used to expand sales and production capacity for the innovative ARTUS preventive exoskeleton, which aims to improve ergonomics and occupational safety in manual work processes.

Since 2019, the founding team has been working on exoskeletons for the hands to address an unsolved problem: more than 30% of workplace accidents involve the hand. Thanks to the EXIST research transfer programme of the Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Climate Action (BMWK), the team was able to use its scientific findings and prototypes to enter the market. The company was founded in 2023 and is already marketing its first product, ARTUS. It is a passive exoskeleton that protects the fingers from accidents and from excessive strain on the skin and finger joints. The problem has long gone unsolved, and shortly after its launch ARTUS was awarded the Ergonomics Prize 2024.

Viola Bartels, a trained orthopaedic technician and Managing Director, is delighted with the success of the financing round: “At Digity, we believe in the power of our hands – our most important tools in everyday life. Our mission is to keep them healthy, protected and productive at all times. With our technologies, we can for the first time equip hands with both rigid, that is, solid, and freely movable exoskeletons. This seed financing puts us in a position to roll out our ARTUS product on a large scale and develop it further at the same time.”

“With ARTUS, Digity has developed a unique exoskeleton for the fingers with great potential for use in both industry and logistics, and already has further products in the pipeline. That is what convinced us,” says Thomas Müller, Investment Manager at NBank Capital.

“My father, a window builder, already knew the problem that textile gloves cannot solve. I was all the more impressed by how well the Digity team addresses this issue with its innovative exoskeleton, thereby making an important contribution to better prevention in occupational safety. In addition, the founding team has diverse ties to the Thuringian Eichsfeld region,” adds Stefan Jahn, Senior Investment Manager at bm|t in Thuringia.

Dr Martha Smets, Investment Manager at Life Science Valley Ventures, is impressed by the rapid market success: “Digity has managed to bring a strong product with high demand to market in a complex regulatory environment. In the life sciences, this is a remarkable milestone, as such early proof of market is rare and is already generating significant economic impact. Early market acceptance and the economic success that comes with it underline the importance of investing in the life sciences and of promoting innovation. This strengthens Göttingen as a hub for science and business.”

“With our commitment, we want to help ensure that this innovative product from Lower Saxony continues to establish itself in the market over the long term and becomes an indispensable aid in the working world. We believe in the possibilities that ARTUS and its technologies offer,” explains Fabrizio Mangano, Investment Manager at MBG, on the decision.

For the business angels, the founding team was the decisive factor. “In interdisciplinary teams, we develop holistic solutions through open discussion. Seeing how the Digity team interacts with customers, partners and investors on a basis of trust and respect demonstrated strong execution power and a pronounced entrepreneurial mindset. That is ultimately what convinced us,” says Christiane Hoffmann.

Managing Director Claudio Garcia is likewise looking forward to the next steps: “The know-how and capital allow us to focus fully on our business. The mix of investors is ideal for mastering the challenges ahead. Alongside internationalisation, we now turn to sales to medium-sized and large companies, which usually have long processes that need to be navigated.”

The exoskeletons are already in use across a range of industries, whether in the installation of insulation or on the production lines of automotive and household appliance manufacturers. “Individually tailored trial phases and customised solutions for different requirements are the key to successful implementation, in order to keep the workforce healthy and productive,” stresses Viola Bartels.

About Digity GmbH

Digity GmbH is a company in the field of exoskeleton technology. Founded in 2023 as a spin-off of the University Medical Center Göttingen, Digity develops and produces innovative solutions to improve ergonomics and occupational safety in a variety of industries. Its first product, ARTUS, is a passive exoskeleton designed specifically for the fingers and has already received multiple awards.
