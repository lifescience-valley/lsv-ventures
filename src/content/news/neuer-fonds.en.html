State of Lower Saxony, Sartorius and University Medical Center Göttingen (UMG)
launch “Life Science Valley” Growth Fund

Strong tailwind for life science start-ups in Göttingen: the State of Lower Saxony, Sartorius AG and the University Medical Center Göttingen are launching the “Life Science Valley” Growth Fund for early-stage financing of start-ups in the life sciences. In its first stage, the fund is endowed with around EUR 12 million of venture capital and is to be expanded to up to EUR 20 million through the addition of further investors. The fund’s investment targets are science-driven start-ups headquartered in Lower Saxony in the fields of biotechnology, medical technology, digital health, data science and drug development.

The fund is managed by Life Science Valley Ventures Management GmbH, whose Managing Directors are Marco Janezic, an investor and serial founder, and Dr Sven Wagner, an experienced industry expert. The State is contributing around EUR 6 million to the fund via NBank, while a further EUR 6 million in equity in total comes from Sartorius and the University Medical Center Göttingen. The “Life Science Valley” Growth Fund is one of three regional growth funds in Lower Saxony in which the State holds a significant financial stake. The other two are managed from Hannover and Osnabrück.

“Göttingen benefits from the great commitment of Sartorius AG and the University Medical Center and has developed into a centre for young life science companies. This radiates out to the region and beyond. The new fund is an ideal complement to existing support such as the high-tech incubator, which helps young talents at the university turn their ideas into business models. We need young minds, especially in the life sciences, to continue on the path towards a liveable and sustainable Lower Saxony,” says Olaf Lies, Lower Saxony Minister for Economic Affairs, Construction, Transport and Digitalisation.

Dr Ulf Meier, Member of the Executive Board of NBank, which implemented the growth fund with State funds on behalf of the State and is participating in the new fund as a limited partner: “NBank very actively supports the development of Göttingen and the entire region into a life science stronghold. After the funding of the Life Science Factory and the high-tech incubator, the Life Science Valley Fund is the third building block benefiting Göttingen as a life science location. In total, this amounts to around EUR 13 million in funding and EUR 6 million in equity capital. The high-tech incubator is currently supporting 15 start-up teams. With the new fund, there is now a good financing perspective for the teams that are developing promisingly in the high-tech incubator. We see a lot of potential here locally, but the fund naturally has start-ups from all over Lower Saxony in its focus.”

The metropolitan region is a strong driver of innovation when it comes to new ideas in the life sciences, says Prof. Wolfgang Brück, Member of the Executive Board of the University Medical Center Göttingen: “At UMG, translation, that is, the transfer of scientific findings into application, is a major concern for us, so that new active substances and innovative procedures can bring added value to patients as quickly as possible. We are aware of the particular challenges facing life science start-ups, and not least the necessary seed capital is a decisive factor for success. As a pioneer from the scientific sphere, we as a university medical center want to make our contribution.”

For many years, the biotechnology group Sartorius has been a key driver of location development and innovation promotion in Göttingen, engaging in various start-up initiatives both regionally and internationally. “With its renowned research institutions and resident industry, Göttingen is already an attractive and innovative location today. But first-class technologies and innovative strength will not help even the best start-up if there is a lack of founding and growth capital. This applies in particular to the life sciences, which are characterised by very long development times and high investment needs. With our participation in the regional growth fund, we are supporting an important step towards overcoming this hurdle,” says Dr Joachim Kreuzburg, CEO of Sartorius.

About Life Science Valley Ventures Management GmbH
Life Science Valley Ventures Management GmbH was founded in 2022 to advance regional investment activities in Göttingen and Lower Saxony. The company is based in Göttingen and is led by Dr Sven Wagner and Marco Janezic.
